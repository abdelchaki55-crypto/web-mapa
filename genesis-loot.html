EXPERIMENTAL ESTIMATES How to read the percentages

Percentages estimate the chance of at least one matching reward per mission completion in the Beacon loot model. They are not measured live-game drop rates. Mission-specific give rules, patches and server settings can change the outcome. Difficulty can change reward quality while leaving the modeled drop chance unchanged. A blueprint percentage is the chance of receiving at least one blueprint, not the blueprint share of an already awarded item.

The model uses the exported item-set weights, selection counts, quantities and duplicate settings, following Beacon’s simulator. All Genesis mission tables in this snapshot are marked experimental by Beacon. Unmodeled values appear as “—”.
